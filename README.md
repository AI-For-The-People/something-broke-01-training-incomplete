**A free technical puzzle by [AI For The People](https://www.youtube.com/@AI-For-The-People).**

## The Situation

Your employer requires proof that you've completed mandatory employee training.

Unfortunately, one of the training videos refuses to play.

You need to complete your training and submit the required confirmation.

That's all you have to work with.
